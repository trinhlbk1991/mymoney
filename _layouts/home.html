FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it work with my bank?

If your bank or e-wallet app shows a notification when you pay, yes. myMoney reads the text, so it doesn't need a list of supported banks, and it reads notifications in any language.

Does auto-capture work on iPhone?

Yes, on iOS 27 or later. You set up one Shortcuts automation, and the app walks you through it with screenshots. On Android, you allow notification access and you're done.

What if it gets a payment wrong?

Every field stays editable. If you'd rather check first, turn on review and each capture waits in To review until you confirm it. You can also tie keywords to a category so a merchant always lands in the same place.

What happens after the 3 free captures?

Payments still land in To review, marked Premium only, and wait there unread. Buy Bánh Xèo and they're all read, so nothing is lost if you decide later.

Which languages and currencies?

The app is in English and Vietnamese and works with any currency, from the đồng to the dollar, euro or yen.
