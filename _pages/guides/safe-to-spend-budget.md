## The formula

**Safe to spend today = (monthly budget − spent so far this month) ÷ days left, counting today**

## A worked example

Say your monthly spending budget is **9,000,000₫** and it's the 21st of a 30-day month.

| | |
|---|---|
| Spent so far | 6,200,000₫ |
| Left | 9,000,000 − 6,200,000 = **2,800,000₫** |
| Days left (21st to 30th) | **10** |
| Safe to spend today | 2,800,000 ÷ 10 = **280,000₫** |

You spend 180,000₫ today. Tomorrow you have 2,620,000₫ left over 9 days, so your safe-to-spend goes **up** to about 291,000₫. The 100,000₫ you didn't spend is shared across the rest of the month.

## Why a daily number works

- **It's small enough to remember.** "280k today" is easier to keep in mind than "2.8 million for the rest of the month".
- **It shows overspending right away.** A big weekend shows up as a smaller number on Monday, not as a surprise on the 30th.
- **It rewards a quiet day.** Spend less and the next days get more.

## Setting a budget you'll keep

1. **Start from income.** Take your monthly take-home pay.
2. **Take out savings first.** Decide what goes to savings or goals before anything else.
3. **Take out fixed bills.** Rent, utilities, phone, subscriptions, loan payments.
4. **What's left is your spending budget.** That's the number the daily amount comes from.
5. **Add category caps where you overspend.** If food delivery or shopping keeps running hot, give it its own limit.

## How myMoney does it

Set one monthly budget and myMoney shows your **safe to spend per day** at the top of the budget screen, with how much is left and how many days to go. It updates the moment a payment is logged, and with [auto-capture](/auto-capture/) that's as soon as your bank's notification arrives. Category budgets show which one is running hot, and goals tell you how much to put aside each month.
