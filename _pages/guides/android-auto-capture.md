## Before you start

- **myMoney** from Google Play
- Your bank or e-wallet app with **payment notifications turned on**

## Step by step

1. **Install myMoney** and open **auto-capture** in Settings.
2. **Allow notification access.** Android opens its own permission screen. Turn myMoney on there, then come back to the app.
3. **Pick your bank and e-wallet apps.** Add each banking and payment app you use. myMoney reads only these apps' notifications, and only the ones with a number in them.
4. **Choose review or auto-save.** With review on, each capture waits in **To review** until you confirm it. With it off, captures save on their own.
5. **Pay as usual.** The next payment notification becomes a transaction with the amount, merchant, category and wallet filled in. Promotions and OTP codes are skipped.

## Tips

- **Card digits pick the wallet.** Add the last 3–4 digits of each card to its wallet.
- **Keyword rules fix categories for good.** Tie a merchant's name to a category once, and it always lands there.
- **Battery savers.** If captures stop arriving, check that your phone's battery optimisation isn't stopping myMoney in the background.
- **Your first 3 captures are free.** After that, [Bánh Xèo](/pricing/) reads every payment.
