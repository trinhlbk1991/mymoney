# VERSION 7.0.0
**Pay as usual. It’s already written down.**

Auto-capture reads the payment notifications from your bank and e-wallet apps, and Gemini AI turns each one into a transaction. No typing.

- Reads only the bank and e-wallet apps you pick
- Puts each payment in the right wallet by its card digits
- Check each one first, or let it save on its own
- Your first 3 captures are free
- Works on Android, and on iPhone (iOS 27+) through one Shortcuts automation
- Currency, wallets, categories and recurring now have their own group in Settings.
- Going Premium happens on one sheet, with every step in view.

# VERSION 6.1.0
**Add a spend from any tab.**

A new floating tab bar keeps the add button one tap away, without covering your list.

- The add button sits beside the tab bar on every tab
- Pick from 18 categories without scrolling
- Tap a memo suggestion to fill it in
- Lists scroll clear of the tab bar, so the last row is never hidden. New goals start from the “+ Goal” button in the Goals header.

# VERSION 6.0.0
**Give your money a goal.**

Choose a target, set your pace, and see what’s left to save.

- Plan at your pace
- Make it yours with goal icons
- See only what’s still due this month
- Clearer report and budget amounts.
