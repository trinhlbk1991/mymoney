## Why it's called Bánh Xèo

Bánh xèo is a crispy Vietnamese pancake, sold from street stalls all over the country. myMoney is made in Ho Chi Minh City, and like the dish, Premium is a small one-off treat rather than a bill every month.

## Try before you buy

The 3 free auto-captures are there so you can see it read your own bank's notifications before paying anything. Payments that arrive after those 3 wait in **To review**, marked Premium only. If you buy Bánh Xèo later, they're all read, so nothing is lost.
