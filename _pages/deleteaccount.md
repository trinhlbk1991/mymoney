## App information

myMoney by Iced Tea Labs, distributed on the Google Play Store.

[{{ site.playstore_link }}]({{ site.playstore_link }})

## How to delete myMoney's account from the app

First, click on **Profile** tab:

<img src="{{ '/assets/delete-1.png' | relative_url }}" width="250" alt="myMoney Profile tab">

Scroll to the bottom, click on **DELETE ACCOUNT** button:

<img src="{{ '/assets/delete-2.png' | relative_url }}" width="250" alt="Delete account button at the bottom of the Profile tab">

Finally, confirm that you want to delete account and all the associated data:

<img src="{{ '/assets/delete-3.png' | relative_url }}" width="250" alt="Delete account confirmation dialog">

## Data that will be deleted

- Account information (email, phone number, account id) on Firebase Auth.
- All the associated data: backups on Google Drive.
